Jomo: Your AI Travel Agent

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Jomo is operated by MRDD OÜ, Estonia, the controller of personal data described here. For privacy questions and requests, contact contact@mrdd.io.

Data used to run Jomo

  • Account information: your email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication information needed for the sign-in method you choose.
  • Trip and group content: plans, dates, places, notes, membership and permissions, RSVP and attendance answers, chat messages, polls, and files or photos you choose to attach. Content in a shared plan is available to its authorized members.
  • Expenses and debts: amounts, currencies, descriptions, payers, balances, and receipts you add. Receipts attached to a shared plan are uploaded so its members can access them. Private debts are associated with the relevant accounts. Jomo does not transfer money or collect payment-card details.
  • Location: the position you choose to share with a plan, after granting device permission. The shared position may be precise. Jomo does not continuously track your location in the background.
  • Notifications: device push tokens and notification preferences, used to deliver plan updates and messages.

We use this information to provide the features you request, synchronize shared plans, manage access, and keep the service secure. Our legal bases are performance of the service contract, consent for optional processing, and legitimate interests in security and responding to support requests, as applicable.

AI assistant and Anthropic

The optional plan assistant uses Claude, provided by Anthropic. Before sending an AI request, Jomo asks for your permission. It sends your question and relevant conversation context, the plan’s name, dates, events, places, cities and notes, your role, and your own RSVP answers. This information is used to answer your request and, when available, propose changes for you to review.

We do not deliberately include account identifiers or email addresses in AI context. However, text you or other plan members enter may itself contain personal information. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. The assistant may use web search, and relevant search queries are processed by the AI provider’s search service.

Jomo stores your assistant conversation, any proposals, consent decisions, and technical usage records for functionality and cost control. Assistant conversations are private to the account that created them. You can withdraw consent in Profile → Privacy; withdrawing consent stops future AI requests and does not undo processing already performed.

Optional diagnostics

Where enabled for your app version and with your preference turned on, usage analytics records coded app actions in our own database, and crash reports are processed by Sentry. Product events can be associated with your account and plan but are not intended to include message contents, event titles, names, or email addresses. Crash diagnostics include technical error and device information. You can change these preferences under Profile → Privacy.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions; Apple or Google for the sign-in methods you choose; and Expo with Apple’s or Google’s notification services for push delivery. Anthropic processes optional AI requests, and Sentry processes crash diagnostics when enabled. Support email is processed by our email service provider.

Our primary app database is hosted in the EU. This does not mean all processing remains in the EU: AI requests, authentication, notifications, support, and other provider operations may involve other countries. Where required, transfers use applicable safeguards such as contractual protections. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising tracking.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and plan information while needed to provide the service, honor your choices, protect against abuse, and meet legal obligations. Some plan activity is automatically cleaned up under the app’s retention rules; archived plan documents may remain until the plan or relevant account is deleted. Copies already received by other users or stored in their own devices are not under our control. Technical backups may take additional time to expire.

To delete your account, open Profile → Manage account → Delete account. Account deletion removes account-associated records through the app’s deletion process. The effect on shared plans depends on your role and whether other members still use the plan. Contact us if you need help deleting data or cannot access your account.

Your choices and rights

You can manage device permissions, notification preferences, optional diagnostics, and AI consent. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of your data, or object to processing. Contact contact@mrdd.io; we may verify your identity and respond within the applicable legal timeframe. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority or Estonia’s Data Protection Inspectorate.

Security

We use encrypted network connections and account- and membership-based access controls. Group chat is not end-to-end encrypted. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Jomo is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They do not embed analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process standard connection information to operate and secure its hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Updates

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice or request consent where required.
