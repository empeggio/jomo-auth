Jomo: Your AI Travel Agent

How can we help?

Questions about your trip, account, or Jomo? Contact the team.

Email Jomo support

Report a problem

Tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and your device and app version. You can attach a screenshot after removing any personal information. Never send your password, verification codes, or your plan’s join password.

Account access

Use the same sign-in method you originally used. For an email account, use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen. If you still cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your account.

Delete your account or request your data

In Jomo, open Profile → Manage account → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, email contact@mrdd.io. We may need to verify account ownership before responding to a data request.

AI suggestions

The plan assistant can make mistakes. Check opening hours, prices, travel requirements, and bookings directly with the relevant provider before relying on a suggestion. You can withdraw AI permission in Profile → Privacy.

Report content or abuse

For inappropriate content or misuse, email us with enough context to identify the problem. Avoid sharing other people’s private information unnecessarily.
